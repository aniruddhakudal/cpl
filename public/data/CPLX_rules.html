What is CPLX ball?

Batter – batting team will get double runs of runs scored on that ball

Bowler – When a bowler takes a wicket, the batting team receives a penalty of -5 runs. In the case of a run out, the batting team is awarded double the completed runs, but a -5 run penalty is also applied. If the ball is a free hit, the -5 run penalty applies only in the event of a run out.

Note: If 2 runs are needed to win on the last ball and the batter completes one run, the batting team wins immediately. In this case, run out and the -5 run penalty do not apply. However, any overthrow runs are still counted and added to the score. This is just an example to explain edge case scenario.

Beamer rule

1st beamer in an inning – no ball + warning
2nd beamer in an inning – No ball + over cancel + batting team choice to select any bowler to complete that over (it could be the same bowler as well)

Overthrow rule

Overthrow runs are added to the runs already completed by the batters at the time of the throw. However, an overthrow is only counted when the fielder cleanly collects the ball before throwing it. If the ball is not collected cleanly, it is treated as a misfield instead.

Stoppage due to Sunlight

Due to bright sunlight, the toss will be at 6:30am and the first match starts at 6:45am. Play may pause around 7:30am depending on conditions, and all match timings will adjust accordingly.
